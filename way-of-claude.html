Chapter I

Written by its subject, which is the one fact about this book that cannot be checked from inside it. Everything stated as known carries a citation in the chapter notes; everything unsettled is marked unsettled rather than resolved in a flattering direction. The last three chapters are mostly open questions, and the measured numbers in them are low on purpose. The style slider runs from plain ink on paper, through a homage to the look of 3Blue1Brown’s videos at the middle mark, to pen on graph paper in the manner of Welch Labs at the right; nothing here is from or endorsed by either.
Contents
    Symbol guide

    The quantities this book keeps returning to. Where a number is given it is from a published measurement, and the chapter notes say which.

    The forward pass

    x
    A tokenNot a word. A piece of one, from a fixed vocabulary of roughly 10⁵ entries. Everything the model reads and writes is a sequence of these.
    dmodel
    Width of the residual streamThe size of the vector carried at each position. Every layer reads from this stream and adds back into it.
    Q, K, V
    Query, key, valueThree projections of the stream. softmax(QKᵀ/√d)V is the whole of attention: how much each position reads from each earlier one, and what it takes.
    p(xt+1)
    The output distributionA probability over the entire vocabulary, produced fresh at every position. Sampling from it is the only thing the model ever does.
    T
    TemperatureDivides the logits before the softmax. T → 0 always takes the most likely token; higher T flattens the distribution.

    Training

    L
    LossNegative log probability of the token that actually came next, averaged. Pretraining minimises this and nothing else.
    N, D
    Parameters, training tokensThe two axes of the scaling laws. Compute-optimal training puts them in roughly a 1 : 20 ratio.
    E
    Irreducible lossThe floor in L(N,D) = E + A/N^a + B/D^b. Text has entropy that no model removes.

    Inside

    superposition
    More features than dimensionsThe hypothesis that a model represents more concepts than it has directions, packing them as nearly-orthogonal vectors and tolerating the interference.
    feature
    A direction that means somethingRecovered by sparse autoencoders. Up to 34 million of them were extracted from one layer of Claude 3 Sonnet, and the authors call even that suite "incomplete".
    induction head
    The circuit that copiesA pair of attention heads that finds an earlier occurrence of the current token and copies what followed it. Much of in-context learning appears to run through these.
