PAUL'S GAME GARAGE

How things move
For grown-ups. Marble Maze: touch the board where the marble should go and the board tips that way, up to 11°. The marble speeds up at 5/7 · g · sin θ, the same rolling physics as Ramp Race, and bounces off the walls. The goal is the hole with the yellow rim and flag. The plain dark holes sit only in squares off the route to the goal; rolling into one sends the marble back one square behind the furthest point it reached on the route, not to the start. Mazes start 3 × 3 with one hole and grow by one size, and one hole, every two wins, up to 6 × 6 with four. Ramp Race: drag a ramp's knob to set its slope, tap a ball to swap big and small, press the red button. The steeper ramp always wins, and ball size makes no difference.
