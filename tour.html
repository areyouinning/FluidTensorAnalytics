How do you build a sound?

Twenty-eight tracks, each in the manner of a producer, from Brian Eno's drifting loops to EPROM's machine bass, including three remixes of Bach's Prelude in C. Every sound is written from code: no samples, no instruments, no recordings. Press play and the tour walks you through what to listen for.

The water is stirred by the track: low notes kick up pairs of swirls, held middle notes stir it round, and high notes poke it. Spin lifts the surface; color splits the motion into spin and stretch, and each stop in the tour changes which is shown. The log z button unrolls the box around its centre: angle runs across, turning as the music plays, and distance from the centre runs up on a log scale, so every ring of the water gets the same height and small swirls keep their shape.

The strip is the track drawn as its own transform: time runs left to right, pitch runs up from C1 to C7, and brighter means louder. Tap anywhere to jump.
