Dear kindred stranger,

You found the road, so you're already halfway to Paradox. Write down what you saw, what the creek was doing, and what you'd tell the next person who comes down this road. I'll keep your letter dry until they get here.

Nobody here needs a heart, a brain or courage from you. A few honest words will do.
