Okay data, now let's get in formation

an exploratory explainer for the water-curious gamer

Rain is falling into a tank of water below, and each drop curls into a pair of swirls. The colors split how the water moves into its spinning part and its stretching part: the two halves of a tensor. This whole site is open source and in the public domain: take any of it, for anything. Everything this demo uses is explained somewhere on it, from what a tensor is to why stirring mixes. Explore long enough and you can build it yourself.
