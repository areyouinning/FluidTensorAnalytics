Kiddo’s Puzzle Box

For the grown-up

Four puzzles for someone who can’t read yet. Tap a picture at the top to choose a puzzle, listen to the question, then tap your answer.

Nothing here needs reading, and a wrong tap costs nothing: the game says “try again” and waits. Each puzzle is a small version of an idea from the rest of the site.

What comes next is pattern-spotting: find the bit that repeats. Which one is different is sorting by one feature at a time: color first, then shape, then size. What goes in the gap is a tiny logic grid, where each row and column holds one of each shape, the same rule as a sudoku. Make it balance is counting on: how many more does the light side need?

Every third right answer in a puzzle makes that puzzle one step harder. Progress is kept on this device only; “Start again from easy” clears it.

The voice is River, the same voice that reads in Sound It Out. The puzzles are made fresh each time by your browser.
