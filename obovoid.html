Inspirations

If I have seen further, it has been from the basket of the balloons lifted by the luminaries listed here.

Some of these people taught me across a chalkboard. Some I have only read. Some are gone, and their balloons are in the wind: they lean and drift a little further than the rest. All of them taught this site how to think. Each is an obovoid, egg-shaped with the broad end up: part lightbulb for the idea, part hot-air balloon for the lift. Tap one.

If the site has a house style — follow the arithmetic all the way down, but never lose the delight that made you ask — it was learned here, from them.
