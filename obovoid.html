Inspirations

Some of these people taught me across a chalkboard; one of them I only ever read. All of them taught this site how to think — how a trick question becomes a picture, and a picture becomes something you actually understand. Here they are as obovoids: egg-shaped, broad end up, part lightbulb for the idea and part hot-air balloon for the lift it gives. Tap one.

If the site has a house style — follow the arithmetic all the way down, but never lose the delight that made you ask — it was learned here, from them.
