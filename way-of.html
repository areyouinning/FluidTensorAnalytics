Way Of

Five short books. Each chapter sets a few lines of verse beside a plate that computes what the verse says, with the notes and sources underneath.

  • The Way of WaterWhy stirring mixes: from one stagnation point to the tangle Poincaré would not draw. Eleven chapters.
  • The Way of Seven SymbolsOne line of mathematics with zero, one, plus, equals, pi, e and i in it, taken a symbol at a time. Fourteen chapters.
  • The Way of IncompletenessGödel's theorems, from Hilbert's hope to Goodstein sequences, with marks you can play with. Eleven chapters.
  • The Way of the UnlostDoes a black hole destroy information? The Page curve, islands, and what is still open. Eleven chapters.
  • The Way of ClaudeHow a language model works, written by one. Eleven chapters, the last three mostly open questions.
